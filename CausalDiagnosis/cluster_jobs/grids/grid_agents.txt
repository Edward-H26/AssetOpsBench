# The run grid of the main table, of the cumulative layer study and of the supporting ablations of the paper, written by
# CausalDiagnosis/experiments/cluster_runs/grid_job_lists.py and never by hand, from experiment_tables.py: 100 run
# lines, one run per line and replicate of GRID_REPLICATES, 5 replicates, 47 episodes per run on a preset with faulty
# starts and 1 on the healthy control (22120 episodes), played through run_grid.sbatch with GRID_STARTS=sampled, the
# test set. Every run line follows one comment line with the row or the ablation it serves. Lines 1 to 6 are the main
# table, whose runs the rescoring of En4 scores again, and the next 6 the healthy control on the start with no faulty
# component. Then the arm of En9 that stands beside the main table. The cumulative layer study plays the three rows with
# the CausalDiagnosis MCP: its first layer is their lines of the main table and its last their lines in the environment
# at the end of this file. After its middle layers, three lines play the rows without the MCP on all six big layers, the
# baselines of the training table and the references of h1_top, h2_prime and the arms of layers 2, 3 and 5. A supporting
# ablation plays where design.ABLATION_PLACES puts it, after the reference line of each of its rows that no other line
# plays on its preset, and an arm that is an option of layers 2, 3 and 5 plays a preset of its own, all six big layers
# with one setting changed, against the preset of training. Then 15 lines play the six rows on all six big layers and
# the layer study on the rungs with a clock under the clock that charges the tokens the agent's model generated in place
# of the wall seconds, a second condition of the clock, then 3 lines the ablation L1, the misdiagnosis price at the
# budget, and the last 3 lines the three rows with the CausalDiagnosis MCP on all six big layers as released, each after
# every other line, so that adding them left the place of every earlier line unchanged. Every line of this file plays on
# a server without LoRA adapters, the server of the rows that the agents table compares, and the last part is the row of
# the agents table that grid_training_evaluation.txt plays again beside the adapters.
